The Long Meander

Key G · ♩=76 · 4/4
Down where the willows bend to drink,
the water keeps the ledger of the shore.
Every name we carved into the bank
the river reads, and carries to the sea.
Chorus
Roll on, long meander,
take the slow way home.
What the current gathers,
the delta gets to know.
I have hurried half my mornings gone,
chasing after trains I never caught.
The river never hurried anywhere
and still it finds the ocean every time.
— chorus —
So lay your worry down among the reeds,
let the water hold the things you cannot keep.
We are all just rain that got together,
looking for the long way to the sea.
— chorus ×2 · end on G —

Last Bus Out of Calloway

The diner light goes amber
at a quarter after nine,
Earl stacks up the chairs
and pockets up his keys.
There's a duffel in the gravel
and a ticket in my hand,
and a wind that smells like
anywhere but here.
Mama said this town will hold you
like a too-small coat,
warm enough to make you
stay too long.
I left a note by the coffee pot
with the truth and nothing else:
some birds are only
borrowing the barn.
Chorus
Last bus out of Calloway,
headlights through the rain.
Everything I'm keeping
fits the rack above my seat.

The Orchard Waltz

Key D · ♩=96 · 3/4
Take down the lantern that hangs in the rafter,
shake out your Sunday-best shawl.
The cider is sweet and the fiddle is tuning
in the orchard at the end of the fall.
Chorus
Spin with me under the apple boughs, darling,
leaves coming down where we turn.
All of the winters that wait for us, darling,
can wait till the lantern burns down.
Your grandmother danced here the year of the comet,
your grandfather counted her steps.
The trees keep the time of a hundred Octobers
in rings that nobody has read.
— chorus —
When the band packs away and the lantern goes under
and frost finds the gate by the lane,
we'll have had this one waltz at the turn of the season,
and nobody takes it away.
— chorus · ritard, end on D —
