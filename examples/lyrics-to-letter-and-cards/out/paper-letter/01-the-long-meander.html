The Long Meander

Key G♩ = 764/4fingerpick or flat
Down where the willows bend to drink,
the water keeps the ledger of the shore.
Every name we carved into the bank
the river reads, and carries to the sea.
Chorus
Roll on, long meander,
take the slow way home.
What the current gathers,
the delta gets to know.
I have hurried half my mornings gone,
chasing after trains I never caught.
The river never hurried anywhere
and still it finds the ocean every time.
— chorus —
So lay your worry down among the reeds,
let the water hold the things you cannot keep.
We are all just rain that got together,
looking for the long way to the sea.
— chorus, twice · end on G, let it ring —
