Chicken · Preserved Lemon · Olives

425°F
1¼ HR
total · serves 4
  1. 1SALT thighs. Rest 30 min, or overnight.
  2. 2SEAR skin-down, cold dry pan, 7–8 min. Don't move them.
  3. 3FLIP 2 min; set aside. Keep 2 tbsp fat.
  4. 4SOFTEN onion 5 min; garlic + spices 1 min.
  5. 5ADD stock, lemon, olives — scrape the brown bits free.
  6. 6NESTLE thighs skin-up, above the liquid.
  7. 7OVEN uncovered 40–45 min, till mahogany.
  8. 8REST 10 min. Parsley over; bread for the juices.

Ingredients

  • 8 chicken thighs bone-in, skin-on
  • 1 tbsp kosher salt
  • 1 preserved lemon rind only, slivered
  • 1 cup green olives pitted + smashed
  • 1 yellow onion thin half-moons
  • 4 garlic cloves sliced
  • 1½ tsp ground coriander
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1½ cups chicken stock
  • handful parsley chopped
  • crusty bread for the pan juices
